# Register and get your personal code

## Register

Open **Register** (or `/students/register`) and fill in the fields your teacher chose: usually your name, your email and some details of your machine. Under a field you may find a short help written by your teacher.

{% include screenshot.html file="students-register" alt="Registration form" %}

{: .tip }
If a field is filled in automatically with your IP, register **from the machine that will be evaluated**. Otherwise ask your teacher to fix it.

If something is wrong, the form tells you which field to fix:

{% include screenshot.html file="students-register-errors" alt="Registration form with errors" %}

- Required fields cannot be empty.
- Emails must look like an email.
- Typed values accept only letters, digits, spaces and `. _ - @ : /` (passwords excepted), up to 100 characters.
- A machine address cannot be the panel's own computer.

## Your personal code

When you register you get a **personal code** and your personal address:

{% include screenshot.html file="students-registered" alt="Registration done: the personal code" %}

**Keep the code**: you need it to run your test and see your results. If you lose it, ask your teacher, who can see every code. Your personal page is `/students/<code>`; you can bookmark it. The browser also remembers your code: the menu shows **My page** instead of **Register**, and the home fills in your code.

## Fix your data

If you typed something wrong, open your page, change the values under **My details** and press **Save my details**. The previous values are replaced. Leave a password field empty to keep the current password.

## Who is registered

The student home lists the names of everyone registered (no codes or other data), so you can check you are in. Your teacher may hide this list.
