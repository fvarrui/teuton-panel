# Student guide

Your teacher gives you an address like `http://192.168.1.10:4567/students`. Open it in a browser, or use `curl` from a terminal: everything works in both.

## In three steps

1. **Register** and keep your personal code (for example `K7QH`).
2. **Run your test** whenever you think a task is done.
3. **Check your results** to see what is still missing, and run again.

Your teacher decides which of these options are available; a disabled one says *Your teacher has not enabled this option*.
